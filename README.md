# PyrenMUN - Model United Nations in the Pyrenees

An innovative diplomatic event that brings together high school students from the three institutions of the Hautes-Pyrénées for an authentic United Nations simulation.

## 🎯 What is PyrenMUN?

PyrenMUN is a collaborative Model United Nations (MUN) conference jointly organized by:
- La Serre de Sarsan High School in Lourdes  
- Victor-Duruy High School in Bagnères-de-Bigorre  
- Théophile Gautier High School in Tarbes

This educational initiative transforms students into diplomats for a day, allowing them to debate major contemporary geopolitical issues in an authentic setting.

## 🌍 The Diplomatic Experience

### For Participants
- **Role-playing as diplomats** representing various countries  
- **Structured debates** on migration issues, geopolitical tensions, and climate challenges  
- **Skill development** in negotiation, argumentation, and international cooperation  
- **Full immersion** in UN procedures  

### Topics Addressed
- Global migration issues  
- Current geopolitical tensions  
- Climate and environmental challenges  
- Contemporary international policies  

## 🎓 Educational Impact

> *"These topics are already discussed at the real United Nations, and we want to debate them too to better understand current policies."* – Lou, 11th grade student

### Educational Objectives
- **In-depth geopolitical understanding** of current global issues  
- **Development of autonomy** and critical thinking  
- **Inter-school collaboration** strengthening regional bonds  
- **Active and engaged citizenship education**  

## 🤝 A Unique Collaboration

The event perfectly illustrates regional educational cooperation, showing how three institutions can join forces to provide a rich and authentic learning experience.

## 📅 Organization

**Duration**: Full day of diplomatic simulation  
**Format**: Interactive conference with debates, negotiations, and resolutions  
**Supervision**: Teachers from the three participating high schools
